How models are tested and ranked

Calibrate scores 14 test indexers whose right answers are known, twice, with thinking off and on where the model can think, and grades the answers out of 100. It then finds how many of your indexers the model handles in one request, the context size it needs, and a timeout. Several minutes per model; the AI is not used for anything else while it runs.

Ranking: the highest score wins; among models within 2 points of it, the one that fits on the graphics card and answers fastest. Use this model applies the settings Calibrate chose. Nothing switches by itself.
