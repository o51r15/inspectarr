Indexer health

Inspectarr watches how each of your indexers behaves (how fast it answers, how often it fails, whether it hands out harmful releases, and how many downloads succeed) and gives it a health score from 0 to 100. It then sets the indexer order in Prowlarr so the healthiest are searched first. See the scores on the Indexers page.

Switch off unhealthy indexers

When an indexer's health stays below for checks in a row, switch it off for hours, then try it again.

You can switch any indexer back on yourself on the Indexers page.

What counts towards the score

Each share is a fraction of the whole score; the four must add up to 1.0.
