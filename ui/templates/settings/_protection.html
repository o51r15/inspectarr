Remediation & Severity
A ceiling on how far Inspectarr may act. It only ever reduces what happens — it never makes the thresholds below more aggressive, and it never changes their stored values.

What happens when a rule matches. Every finding is graded (executables CRITICAL, archives HIGH, undersized primary file HIGH, filename patterns MEDIUM) and the worst one decides the outcome. These two thresholds split that grade into three bands.

Anything below this is recorded but never acted on.
Between the two, catches are quarantined instead of deleted.

0 = hold indefinitely. A held torrent waits for your decision.
A timer expiring means nobody looked — not that the release was bad.

Per-extension overrides (for example treating .rar as LOW) are available in the Raw YAML editor under remediation.severity_overrides.
