AI Features

AI is entirely optional and off by default. Inspectarr scores indexers with a deterministic weighted formula on its own; Ollama only adds an advisory overlay on top, plus narrated notification digests and summaries.

While this is off, none of the settings below are used, no Ollama request is ever made, and AI scoring is unavailable on the Indexers page. Detection, severity grading, quarantine and remediation never use AI and are unaffected either way.
