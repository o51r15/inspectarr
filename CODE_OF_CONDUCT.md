# Code of conduct

Inspectarr is a small homelab project, and everyone who opens an issue, starts
a discussion or sends a pull request is welcome here.

- Be kind and patient. People arrive with very different setups and experience.
- Assume good intent, and critique ideas and code, never people.
- No harassment, insults, or unwelcome personal or sexual attention.
- Keep it on topic, and keep other people's private details private (logs and
  configs often contain addresses and keys: redact them).

Problems can be reported privately to the maintainer through
[GitHub](https://github.com/o51r15) or via the
[security advisory form](https://github.com/o51r15/inspectarr/security/advisories/new)
if it can't wait. Behaviour that breaks these rules may be removed, and
repeated or serious breaches lead to a ban.

This code is inspired by the [Contributor Covenant](https://www.contributor-covenant.org/).
